## 🌒 About the Novel

*The Secrets of CHIAROSCURO: The Fated Heir of Shadows and Light* is a supernatural dark-fantasy story about bloodlines, buried memories, forbidden bonds, and the dangerous price of destiny.

Humanity believes it is alone in the world.

It is not.

Behind familiar cities and ordinary lives, ancient powers continue to move. Vampires maintain their courts, demons pursue their ambitions, and angelic forces guard truths that were never meant to reach humanity.

The wars that shaped the world were not truly ended. They were hidden, erased from history, and left to wait beneath the surface.

At the centre of this hidden world stands **Joseph Valemont**, a half-vampire, half-human heir born from a forbidden union between a vampire king and a human woman.

His past is fractured. His memories conceal more than he understands. And a power buried deep within him threatens to upset the balance between supernatural realms.

When a demonic attack begins to unlock what was sealed in his childhood, Joseph is pulled into a conflict far older than himself. Hunted by powerful enemies, caught between rival supernatural factions, and surrounded by secrets about his own bloodline, he must uncover why his very existence frightens forces that have ruled for ages.

But prophecy does not promise that he will become a saviour.

**It only warns that the world will never be the same.**

Some secrets were buried to protect the world. Others were buried to protect the ones who control it.

## 🩸 Meet Joseph Valemont

**The Fated Child · The Half-Blood Heir**

Joseph is the living consequence of a love that crossed a boundary the supernatural world considered unbreakable.

Neither fully belonging to the human world nor comfortably fitting into the world of immortals, he becomes the centre of a struggle involving ancient bloodlines, royal power, and forces whose motives are rarely what they seem.

His journey is not simply about becoming stronger. It is about discovering who he is, understanding what was taken from him, and deciding what his inherited power will mean for the world around him.

His greatest question is not only:

**"What am I?"**

It is:

**"What will I become when the truth is finally revealed?"**

## 🕯️ A World Behind the Veil

The world of CHIAROSCURO is built around a hidden supernatural history that humanity has been allowed to forget.

- **The First Silence** — Before humanity had names for the divine or the damned, powerful beings already shaped the world. Angels, demons, and vampires each occupy a place in a fragile and often brutal balance.

- **The Hidden War** — Heaven calls its actions justice, Hell calls its own rebellion, and history records only what those in power allow it to remember. The conflict never truly ended; it learned to operate in the shadows.

- **The Broken Covenant** — A forbidden union between immortal blood and human life gives rise to a child whose existence challenges the rules that hold the supernatural world together.

- **The Masquerade** — The supernatural did not disappear as humanity modernised. Castles became corporate towers, kingdoms learned new forms of influence, and power adapted to wear ordinary faces.

- **The Returning Past** — Sealed memories begin to surface, old alliances grow unstable, and forces that have watched from a distance start moving again.

In this world, the past is never truly gone.

**It is waiting for the right moment to return.**

## 🌓 Why "CHIAROSCURO"?

Chiaroscuro is an artistic term describing the dramatic contrast between light and darkness.

In this story, that contrast is more than an aesthetic. It is the foundation of its world, characters, and conflicts.

The truth may look different depending on who tells it. A saviour to one kingdom may be a threat to another. A legacy praised as noble may conceal generations of cruelty. And the one feared as a monster may be the only person willing to challenge a corrupt order.

Light can deceive.

Darkness can protect.

And the truth may exist somewhere between the two.

**In CHIAROSCURO, darkness does not always mean evil—and light does not always mean good.**
